How GlowBook works

Quote Builder prices each light run off your rate card: run type × feet × storey multiplier, with a peak premium where the roof is steep or cut up. Takedown is priced per foot; storage is a flat per-client fee. Your installer labor cost per foot and product cost come out of the quote to show the job's real profit before you save it.

Season Book tracks every job through quoted → booked → installed → takedown done → stored, with the install window and takedown date on each record.

Renewal List is the money feature: when the season ends, every client is already on next October's call list with their footage, product arrangement, and dates. Work the list, mark booked or passed, and no past client falls through the cracks.

Honest limits: GlowBook is a book, not a messaging or scheduling service — it doesn't text clients, take deposits, or sync between devices. You keep booking and collecting the way you already do; GlowBook keeps the numbers and the list.
